# POC: proof of concept

Two rounds, after POT round 1. Scenarios G1 "switch to us in ten minutes" (built on P1), G2 "pay
from the poster, request from the poster" and G3 "lucky reward at payment", each in a Türkiye and a
UAE variant on dev, against the real ledger container and pinned stubs for the other domains.
Nothing here is started until POT round 1 exits.
